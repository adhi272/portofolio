# Introduce

Bitcoin ($BTC) remains one of the most volatile yet fascinating assets to analyze. For many, the constant price fluctuations seem random, but a deeper look at the weekly chart reveals clear psychological levels where buyers and sellers clash.

In this guide, we will analyze the current weekly chart of **Bitcoin/TetherUS (BTC/USDT)** using core concepts like Support, Resistance, Break of Structure (BOS), and Change of Character (CHoCH) to determine the most probable next direction.

## Core Concepts Explained

Before we dive into the chart, let's define the three key principles:

1.  **Support:** A price level where demand (buying power) is strong enough to prevent the price from falling further. Think of it as a floor.
2.  **Resistance:** A price level where supply (selling power) is strong enough to prevent the price from rising further. Think of it as a ceiling.
3.  **Break of Structure (BOS) & Change of Character (CHoCH):** These are indicators of a shift in market structure. BOS indicates a continuation of a trend, while CHoCH hints at a potential trend reversal.

Here is a breakdown of what the chart tells us:

#### 1. Identifying Major Support Zones (The Floors)

We have two crucial historical support zones identified at the bottom of the chart:

*   **Zone 1 (~$25,000 - $30,000):** This represents a massive historical support level where previous sell-offs were met with intense buying pressure.
*   **Zone 2 (~$55,000 - $60,000):** A more recent and higher support base. This is our major defensive line.

#### 2. Identifying Current Key Resistance (The Ceilings)

The most immediate and critical level to watch is the **historical high zone between $120,000 and $125,000**, labeled with a "Weak High". This is the definitive upper limit for the current market.

#### 3. Analyzing Market Structure Signals (BOS and CHoCH)

This is where the magic happens. On the way up, we see clear labels of **BOS (Break of Structure)**, confirming that each upward move was a continuation of the main bullish trend.

However, notice the small **CHoCH (Change of Character)** label near the $90,000 - $95,000 range. This subtle shift is a crucial warning sign. It suggests that the relentless upward character might be shifting. The inability of the price to maintain momentum after a major high, coupled with a CHoCH signal, often points to an exhaustive phase.

#### 4. The Path Forward: Likely Scenarios

Given the current setup, here is a probable path forward:

*   **Bearish/Correction Case (More Probable):** The price is likely to exhaust itself below the major resistance zones. A corrective move down to test the next higher support (around $60,000 - $65,000) seems very plausible. The recent price action already looks rejected at the immediate upper level.
*   **Bullish Case (Continuation):** For the bull run to continue directly, we need to see the price break above the minor immediate resistance and eventually challenge the Weak High. A clear Break of Structure above $125,000 would invalidate the correction thesis.

### Conclusion

Mastering Support and Resistance is about understanding market psychology. While nothing in trading is 100% certain, levels and structure signals like CHoCH provide high-probability areas for decisions. By combining these, you can get a clear, data-driven view of Bitcoin’s likely path without falling for the noise.
